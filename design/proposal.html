YuE2 Studio: look and feel

Apple-native structure, terracotta from the logo as the only accent. Expression lives in two places: the generation moment and the artwork of each song.

1. Generating

The signature moment. A circular waveform pulses around the mark and fills with terracotta as the engine advances through its real stages. When it finishes, the ring settles and the song's artwork lands in the Library row.
